To let a client sign in, create their account in Supabase Authentication first. Then copy the user's UUID and connect it to their record below. Never ask a client to share their password with you.

  1. Create the user in Supabase → Authentication → Users.
  2. Copy that user's UUID.
  3. Paste the UUID into the client's record and select “Connect account”.
  4. Send the client the link to client.html.
